Product Care Guide

A little care keeps your gear looking (and working) its best. Here's how to look after everything in the GenX Coding catalogue.

Apparel (Hoodies, T-Shirts, Blankets)

  • Turn garments inside out and machine wash cold to protect printed designs.
  • Skip the bleach - use a gentle detergent to keep colors from fading.
  • Air dry or tumble dry on low. High heat can crack or shrink graphic prints.
  • Iron inside out on low heat if needed, avoiding direct contact with the print.

Drinkware (Mugs, Bottles)

  • Hand wash printed mugs and bottles to keep the design looking sharp longer.
  • Avoid abrasive sponges directly on printed areas.
  • Let insulated bottles air dry fully with the cap off to prevent odor buildup.

Keyboards, Mice & Electronics

  • Unplug before cleaning. Use compressed air to clear dust from between keys.
  • Wipe surfaces with a lightly damp microfiber cloth - never spray liquid directly onto the device.
  • For deeper cleans, keycap pullers make it easy to remove and rinse keycaps individually.

Desk Accessories & Bags

  • Wipe desk mats with a damp cloth; avoid harsh chemical cleaners on printed surfaces.
  • Spot clean backpacks with mild soap and water, then air dry away from direct heat.
  • Store notebooks and posters flat or rolled (never folded) to avoid creasing.
